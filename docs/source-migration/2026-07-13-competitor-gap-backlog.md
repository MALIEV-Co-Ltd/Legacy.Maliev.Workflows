## Purpose and limits

This backlog converts a dated competitor and SERP review into work that MALIEV can implement and measure. It is not a promise of a particular organic position or an AI Overview/AI Mode citation. Google states that meeting its requirements does not guarantee crawling, indexing, or serving, and that there is no special AI-specific markup required for AI features.

The operating rule is therefore:

> Publish the most useful, verifiable answer for each customer intent; make MALIEV's real operational evidence visible; strengthen local and external authority; then measure query/page cohorts over time.

No item in this backlog authorizes an invented price, tolerance, machine, certification, turnaround time, customer result, project volume, or response-time promise. Competitor claims are recorded as competitor positioning, not as facts MALIEV should copy.

## What Google actually requires

- [Google's AI features guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) says normal SEO fundamentals apply to AI Overviews and AI Mode. A supporting page must be indexed and snippet-eligible; there is no extra AI schema or `ai.txt` requirement.
- The same guidance recommends crawlable pages, findable internal links, textual content, high-quality supporting media, a good page experience, structured data that matches visible content, and current Business Profile information.
- [Google's structured-data policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/sd-policies) require markup to represent visible page content. Schema can improve understanding and eligibility, but it is not a ranking guarantee.
- [Google's people-first content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) emphasizes original, useful content that demonstrates first-hand expertise and gives a reader a satisfying answer.
- [Google Business Profile local-ranking guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091) describes local results as mainly based on relevance, distance, and prominence. Complete information, correct hours, reviews, photos, and references/links can help; no one can request or pay Google for a better local rank.

MALIEV's repository already contains `LocalBusiness`, `Organization`, `Service`, FAQ, canonical, and sitemap work. That is a useful eligibility baseline, not sufficient evidence that Google has indexed the intended version or regards MALIEV as prominent for these queries. The highest-value gaps are therefore exact Thai intent, visible operational proof, local prominence, external references, and current indexed snippets.

### P0 — Visible proof before stronger claims

Schema cannot substitute for evidence a customer can inspect. Add the following reusable modules only after their facts and media are approved:

1. **Real equipment and process:** owner-approved photographs of the actual machine/process, with make/model or working envelope only when verified. Generated images must never be presented as evidence of MALIEV's facility, equipment, staff, work, or customers.
2. **Anonymized project evidence:** problem, permitted input media, process decision, material, inspection method, and outcome. Obtain customer permission or remove identifying details. Never fabricate a case study.
3. **Inspection evidence:** show the actual inspection operation and explain the method. Publish tolerances or acceptance criteria only when they apply to the documented project or a verified service capability.
4. **Deliverable examples:** show what a customer receives—such as part, mesh, CAD reconstruction, or deviation report—only from approved work and with confidential details removed.
5. **Named expertise:** identify the person who wrote or technically reviewed a guide, their relevant role, and a current profile. Do not invent credentials.
6. **Quote-preparation checklist:** file type, physical sample/photos, material, quantity, use environment, critical features, finish, location, and target delivery need. Treat requested timing as an input, not a promised turnaround.
7. **Facility and contact confidence:** consistent address, Monday–Friday 09:00–18:00 hours, LINE as the primary contact option, Messenger retained, and a direct quotation/upload path.

## Definition of success

This backlog is working when MALIEV can show, for each intent cohort:

1. one indexable, canonical owner page with useful visible Thai content;
2. operational proof that a customer and Google can inspect;
3. consistent business/entity information across `www`, Shopify, GBP, and social profiles;
4. relevant internal and external references;
5. reliable weekly Search Console, GBP, GA4, and qualified-lead evidence; and
6. a sustained improvement trend without turning diagnostic clicks into false conversions.

Neither “number one,” first-page placement, local-pack inclusion, nor AI citation is an acceptance criterion that MALIEV or an agency can guarantee. The controllable acceptance criteria are accurate implementation, useful evidence, authority-building work, clean measurement, and continuous iteration from observed results.
