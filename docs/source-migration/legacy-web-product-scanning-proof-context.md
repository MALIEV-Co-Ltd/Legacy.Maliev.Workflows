# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Engineers and product teams at Thai hardware startups and manufacturers who need prototypes or low-volume production parts — CNC machining, 3D printing, 3D scanning, 3D design, silicone casting, or low-volume injection molding. They arrive with a CAD file or a rough idea and need to know, fast, whether MALIEV can make it, what it costs, and whether the shop can be trusted with the design. Secondary audience: procurement and purchasing staff comparing local suppliers on price, reliability, and lead time rather than engineering nuance.

## Product Purpose

MALIEV is a family-owned custom manufacturing company (founded 2018, Nonthaburi/Bangkok, Thailand) whose mission is to bring manufacturing to the connected world through the internet. The marketing site is the front door for that mission: it explains what MALIEV can make, proves the shop is capable and trustworthy, and moves a visitor toward a quote. Success is either path completing — a self-serve Instant Quotation submission or a Contact/Talk to Us inquiry — since project complexity, not funnel design, decides which one fits a given visitor.

## Positioning

MALIEV is the one-stop shop for custom part manufacturing in Thailand: CNC machining, 3D printing, 3D scanning, 3D design, silicone casting, and low-volume injection molding all under one roof, so a project can move from prototype to production without changing vendors or losing a single point of contact. Every page should reinforce that breadth — no single process should visually crowd out the others.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary and secondary CTA: Instant Quotation (self-serve, upload CAD and get pricing in minutes) and Contact / Talk to Us (human-reviewed project discussion). Both are first-class; neither should be styled as the fallback.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: "One shop, every process, fast quotes."
- Belief ladder a visitor climbs before requesting a quote:
  1. My project needs more than one manufacturing process, and MALIEV has all of them under one roof.
  2. This shop understands engineering — DFM feedback improves my design, it doesn't just execute blindly.
  3. They quote fast and hit the lead times they promise.
  4. My design and data are handled securely and confidentially.
  5. Getting a quote costs nothing and takes minutes — low-risk to try.
- Proof on hand: the owner-cleared cast-housing scan/CAD screenshots support a bounded visual comparison on the scanning service page; see `../docs/seo/2026-09-03-scanning-sample-display-plan.md` for provenance and release scope. They do not establish dimensional accuracy, inspection results, or permission to distribute geometry. Other proof gaps must not be filled with fabricated examples.

## Brand Personality

Precise, technical, trustworthy — confident competence rather than sales flash. The voice matches an engineer talking to another engineer: specific about process, materials, and tolerances rather than vague adjectives. Warmth comes through responsiveness and plain communication ("real people, clear updates"), not through informality or gimmicks.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS template: cream/pastel backgrounds, gradient text, tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows on every section, identical icon-card grids — the saturated AI-landing-page look this brand should never be mistaken for.
- Cheap marketplace / Alibaba-style: clutter, discount badges, urgency banners — undermines a precision-and-trust positioning.
- Stiff corporate enterprise: jargon-heavy copy, stock-photo boardroom imagery, no personality — feels distant from an engineering audience that wants specifics, not platitudes.

## Design Principles

- **Breadth stays visible.** The one-stop-shop story is the whole positioning; no single service (e.g. 3D printing) should dominate a page to the point the other five processes disappear.
- **Show competence, don't just claim it.** Real process photography, specific tolerances/materials/lead times, and DFM guidance carry more weight than marketing adjectives.
- **Every trust claim earns its own proof.** Security, confidentiality, lead-time reliability, and quality control are each separate promises in the copy — give each a concrete detail, not a shared "trusted by engineers" line doing all the work.
- **Two doors, one destination.** Instant Quotation and human contact are equally valid conversions; layout and hierarchy should never quietly favor one over the other.
- **Precision in the interface, not just the copy.** Sloppy spacing, template-default patterns, or inconsistent components contradict a "precision manufacturing" claim faster than any sentence can compensate for.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA baseline across marketing pages and forms (contact, Instant Quotation, account/login): keyboard navigation, sufficient color contrast, and screen reader support. No additional accommodations specified beyond the AA standard.
