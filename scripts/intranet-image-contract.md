The gate inspects the exact locally built image ID and source revision, never starts its inspection container, and streams selected regular files from an exported archive into the verifier. Existing protected-main CI, environment, permissions, scan severity and digest-resolution gates remain required. No consumer is automatically repinned to this producer.

Creation recovery uses the exact generated name, owner nonce, image ID, actual creation time, expiry label and never-started/no-mount state. Uncertain or foreign ownership is preserved and produces a recovery ledger in the job log and step summary. An expiry label does not perform cleanup. An interrupted create with no visible container retains a pending daemon-creation lease; one empty listing cannot prove future absence. The owning run must re-observe after daemon completion, verify the same identities and delete only its exact owned ID. Volume-bearing images refuse before allocation.

Before export, the Linux file-size limit is set/read back at no more than 512 MiB and inherited by the existing 120-second Docker CLI subprocess. The previous limit is restored in finally. At least 1088 MiB observed free space admits up to 512 MiB archive, 512 MiB cumulative selected copies and 64 MiB headroom. The size limit bounds the CLI's archive file, not daemon storage. The space check is not a reservation; concurrent consumption or unavailable limits fails closed. The publisher job retains its existing 30-minute timeout.
